Kartopu Savaşı / Snowball Fight — local prototype asset audit

New visual assets
- All arena, winter characters, held snowballs and pooled particles are original
  procedural Three.js geometry in visual.ts. Labels are locally rendered text.
- No external models, textures, audio samples, image generation, downloaded packs,
  or new third-party asset licenses were introduced.

Audio source
- Existing original procedural recipes in shared/party-lab/feedback/sfx.ts,
  played through the unchanged AudioManager and its 16-voice pool.
- Packing: release + grab. Throw: throw. Surface impact: floorFlop at low intensity.
  Player hit: bodyHit. KO: knockout. Respawn: recovery. All are synthesized.
- No copyrighted recordings or new audio binaries are used.

Reuse audit
- Barn: shoulder aim, camera-ray/world-target conventions, pooled effects.
- Prop Hunt: shoulder offset, body fade near walls, camera collision conventions.
- Bomb Tag: camera-relative sprint/movement and local bot navigation conventions.
- Existing ragdoll controller: inspected; left unchanged because real crouch needs
  a different collision envelope. This mode uses its own dynamic Rapier capsule.
- Shared safe imports: initializePhysics, bindLook (via ArenaScene), AudioManager.
- Local Test Arena: lazy playground, own HUD, 2/3 seats and existing Esc menu.
- Existing Kenney Nature/Prototype/Particle references and shipped kit credits
  were inspected. Their original raw pack directories are no longer at the paths
  recorded by the build scripts. No kit assets were copied into this prototype;
  compact procedural snow scenery avoids loading unrelated mode assets.

All gameplay and presentation additions remain under scene/snowfight/. There is
no server registration, networking implementation, mode-list or protocol change.
