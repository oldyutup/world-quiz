# Renk Kaosu (`color_chaos`)

One floating field of coloured hex tiles. A target colour is announced; when the reaction
timer runs out every tile of another colour drops for a moment, and whoever stands on one
falls. Last player standing wins. Local: 1 human + 1–2 bots ("Yerel Test Arenası → Harita →
Renk Kaosu"). Online (protocol 7): 2–3 players, the room server drives the same game
(`ColorRoundSimulation`); see [../../COLOR_ONLINE.md](../../COLOR_ONLINE.md). The rules below
are the approved ones for both.

## Field

Katman Kaosu's L1 disc on its own: every hex cell whose centre is within 9.2 m of the middle,
**85 tiles**, 2.0 m flat to flat, 0.5 m deep, flush (the 6 cm groove is rendering only),
walking surface y = 0, Ø ≈ 20.7 m, rings 0 (hub) to 5 (rim). Elimination: hips below the
shared fall line (−5 m).

Spawns (pelvis 0.9 m, facing the middle): 3 players on ring 3 toward (6, 0), (−3, ±5.2) —
6 m out, 10.4 m apart; 2 players on ring 3 at (±6, 0), 12 m apart. Never on the rim.

## Colours

Blue (circle), yellow (stripes), green (triangle outline), pink (cross): `#1643d3`,
`#f8d51e`, `#20b270`, `#eb47b3`. Every pair stays ≥ 35 ΔE apart under simulated deutan,
protan and tritan vision (≥ 66 normal) and ≥ 36 from the player colour of the same hue. The
symbol is drawn a shade lighter/darker on the top, so colour is never the only cue. The HUD
names the colour and shows its symbol.

## Layouts

Rules (`LAYOUT_RULES`, checked for every bank layout by the tests):

- balanced: counts within 2 of each other (free layouts: exactly 21/21/21/22);
- clustered: every same-colour group is 2–7 tiles (never a single tile);
- spread: every colour has ≥ 3 separate groups;
- reachable: from **every** tile every colour is ≤ 2 steps away — so from every spawn and
  from wherever a survivor stands after a drop, every target is fair.

`designLayout(seed, order)` anneals from a shuffled, exactly balanced start by swapping two
tiles of different colours (the balance never changes) against a penalty for rule breaks
(plus a mild preference for groups of 3–5). With `order` 2/3 it swaps whole orbits of a
half/third turn, so the layout keeps that rotation symmetry. The build script keeps valid,
distinct (up to symmetry and palette) designs from fixed seeds and writes `layoutBank.ts`;
a test re-derives the first entry of each bank. Result: in the cycle bank a target is
already under a player 25% of the time, one step away 53%, two steps 22%.

- **Opening** (cycle 1, visible through the countdown): a third-turn (3 players) or
  half-turn (2 players) symmetric layout — the spawns are one orbit of that rotation, so
  every player starts on the same colour with every colour at the same distance.
- **Later cycles**: a free layout (never the same bank entry twice in a row).
- Each cycle shows its layout through a random field symmetry (12) and colour permutation
  (24): both keep every rule.

## Target colour

A shuffle bag: each bag holds the four colours once in random order; a bag never starts with
the colour that ended the previous one. So never the same target twice in a row, every
colour once per four cycles, no colour missing for more than six. Everything is drawn from
one seeded PRNG (mulberry32) per round; the match seed is random locally, fixed in tests.

## Cycle (60 Hz round ticks; tick 0 = first playing tick)

| Phase | Length | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Countdown | 3.0 s | Frozen, no punches, every tile standing, cycle-1 colours visible |
| Preview | 0.9 s (none in cycle 1) | New colours; the returning tiles rise back (0.45 s); no target |
| Run | reaction time | Target announced ("MAVİ!"), outlined and pulsing; others grey out and tremble in the last 0.5 s |
| Drop | on one tick | Every non-target collider disabled and parked before that tick's physics; tiles fall/shrink over 0.3 s |
| Unsafe | 1.5 s from the drop | Only the target colour stands; falls resolve (standing → out in ≈ 0.76 s) |
| Restore | on one tick | Every collider back; the next cycle (new layout + target) starts with its preview |

Reaction time by cycle: 1–2: 2.6 s · 3–4: 2.2 · 5–6: 1.8 · 7–8: 1.5 · 9+: 1.2 (never
lower). Cycle length: 4.1 s (cycle 1), then 0.9 + reaction + 1.5 s: 5.0, 4.6, 4.6, 4.2,
4.2, 3.9, 3.9, then 3.6 s. The 1.2 s timer starts with cycle 9 at 34.5 s.

On a restore tick, anyone whose hips are below the walking surface (in a hole, or sliding
down a tile's side, which the controller cannot climb) is out before the tiles come back —
a returning collider never closes around a body.

Round: last alive wins on the tick it happens; the last players out on the same tick draw;
results 3.5 s, then a fresh round (new seed). The shrink's final drop (119.4 s) ends every
round; 125 s is only a safety net (draw).

## Daralma (shrink)

From cycle 19 (70.5 s) the field erodes from the rim toward the middle, on a fixed schedule
(`COLOR_CHAOS.shrink`). Each stage keeps the tiles within a radius of the middle (tile
centres), then "four", then none:

| Cut cycle (starts) | Coloured tiles | Marked in | Tiles gone that cycle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | 85 | — | — |
| 20 (74.1 s) | 73 (≤ 8.8 m) | 19 (70.5 s) | 12 |
| 21 (77.7 s) | 61 (≤ 8.1 m, hex ring ≤ 4) | 20 | 12 |
| 22 (81.3 s) | 43 (≤ 7.0 m) | 21 | 18 |
| 23 (84.9 s) | 31 (≤ 5.3 m) | 22 | 12 |
| 24 (88.5 s) | 19 (ring ≤ 2) | 23 | 12 |
| 26 (95.7 s) | 7 (ring ≤ 1) | 25 | 12 |
| 28 (102.9 s) | 4 ("four": the middle + every other tile around it) | 27 | 3 |
| 32 (117.3 s, drop 119.4 s) | 0 (final drop) | 31 | 4 |

- **Marked** (cycle `cut − 1`): those tiles still play normally but carry a black-and-white
  dashed border that runs around them and a dark body. "DARALIYOR!" shows under the target
  chip for that whole cycle.
- **Grey** (cycle `cut`): the marked tiles that dropped come back without a colour (dark ash,
  no symbol, the dashes faster), stand through the preview and the reaction time, are never
  the target, and drop with the other colours. The marked tiles that were the target (so
  still standing) turn grey the same way.
- **Gone**: a tile leaving for good sinks 9 m tumbling over 0.9 s (a normal drop: 1.4 m in
  0.3 s) and never returns.
So no tile leaves while it is the target, and none a player could stand on leaves without a
full cycle's warning (tested for every cycle of many seeds).

Colours: each disc stage has its own bank (`STAGE_RULES`): balanced, every colour in ≥ 2
patches with one of ≥ 2 tiles (single tiles allowed only from 31 tiles down, where reach
across the grey ring needs them), every colour ≤ 2 steps from every tile — within the stage
and across the previous stage's field (its grey ring stands in the cut cycle). The 7-tile
stage has one design (middle tile its own colour, the ring alternating the other three).
"four" has one tile per colour, so exactly one tile is safe each cycle, wherever the target
is; its tiles are ≤ 2 steps apart through the middle. The final cycle has no colour ("SON!",
"Hepsi düşüyor · en son düşen kazanır"): the last one to fall wins, same tick = draw.

Measured (the physics): from every present tile of every stage the target is ≤ 2 steps; the
farthest 2-step cases (4.0 m centre to centre) are made at 1.2 s sprinting after a read of up
to 0.8 s (0.9 s fails), walking after 0.6 s.

## Movement, punch

Shared ragdoll unchanged: walk 4.6 m/s, Sprint (Shift) the shared ×1.4 with no stamina,
jump unchanged (0.6 s, pelvis +0.96 m). Seams are as smooth as a slab (tested).

Measured momentum grace: a body *running* toward the target when its floor drops keeps going
and is caught by the target's edge if its pelvis is ≤ 1.1 m (walking) / ≤ 1.6 m (sprinting)
short of it; standing still on a wrong tile always falls. Jumping over the drop does not help
(you land in the hole).

Punch (Punch binding): Katman Kaosu's shove as Renk Kaosu's own tuning (`COLOR_CHAOS.punch`,
same values) — 0.6 s cooldown, alternating hands, real hand contact, whole-body Δv 3.0 m/s
horizontal + 0.3 m/s up, 0.35 s stagger (posture 0.7, mobility 0.25, no jump); a hit during
a stagger pushes without extending it. No health, knockout, grab, lift, weapons or traps. A
shove landing in the last ~0.6 s knocks a player standing ≤ 0.6 m from a patch edge off it;
0.8 m inside they hold.

## Camera

Elevated chase camera, centred: pivot 0.9 m over the hips, boom 5.3 m, rest pitch 28°
(12°–60°), 60° FOV, mouse/trackpad orbit (Pointer Lock or drag), WASD camera-relative, the
body turns toward its movement. No collision needed (one floor, nothing overhead). A jump
lifts the view only 15% of the rise. Falling through a hole, the camera stays at standing
height and looks down toward the body (≤ 3.5 m). A body that falls out is watched for 1.2 s,
then the nearest survivor is followed (Q / E cycle, "İzleniyor: …").

## Presentation

- One InstancedMesh for the 85 tiles (1 draw call, 44 triangles each): pale stone body,
  the gameplay colour and symbol on top, target outline + brightness pulse, grey dim and
  last-0.5 s tremble for the rest, the drop (fall 1.4 m, shrink, tilt, 0.3 s) and the rise on
  restore; a white flash on tiles that change colour. Contact shadows under the players
  (1 draw call) mark the support point and vanish over a hole.
- "Prizma Meydanı" background: lilac-white sky and fog, a haze floor above the fall line,
  and 36 generated pieces (low stone prisms, crystal shards, a cloud bank) merged into one
  mesh (1 draw call, 4.2k triangles), all ≥ 32 m from the axis with no top above 6 m.
- HUD: roster, "Tur N", the target chip (swatch + symbol, name, "Bu renge geç!" / "Yerinde
  kal!", reaction timer and bar; "SON!" with a striped swatch in the final drop), the big
  call on each announcement, the "DARALIYOR!" tag, countdown / "Düştün!" /
  result messages, the spectator label, the controls line for the first 5 s, the Esc menu
  (Oyuncu 2/3, Harita, Bakış, Kontroller, Ses, Debug). The menu is not a pause.

## Bots (local)

Same speed and rules as the player, plain movement intent. Per cycle a human-like read of
0.3–0.65 s, and in 15% of cycles a slow one (+0.3–0.6 s); then the best tile of the target
colour by breadth-first search over the tiles that are there (metres + edge and crowd
penalties; a marked tile counts as the edge), about one cycle in eight a worse one; sprint
when walking would be late; straight at the tile when the way is all floor, else via the next
tile on the path; hold the tile's middle while the floor is gone; between cycles wait on a
tile the next cycle keeps, off its edge; shove a rival in front sometimes (mostly near the
deadline), and once safe on the tile with a rival in reach, tap toward them and shove; over a
hole, steer for the nearest standing tile; in the final drop, jump late. They read only what
is shown (marks, colours, timer) — never a future target.

## Round length (deterministic bot rounds, 200 per player count)

| | median | mean | p90 | first elimination (median) | reached the final drop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 bots | 88.0 s | 83.6 s | 109.4 s | 51.8 s | 2.0% |
| 2 bots | 66.2 s | 67.3 s | 99.3 s | 66.2 s | 1.0% |

Without the shrink (same reaction schedule) 23.5% (3 bots) / 12% (2 bots) of rounds ran to
125 s. Bots reach targets better than people (a 0.3–0.65 s read); shoves only move a body
≈ 0.6 m, so the 4-tile finale (≈ 20% of players per cycle) is what ends the long rounds.

## Debug

`?colorDebug=1` (or `?partyDebug=1`) opens the Esc menu's debug panel at start: target,
cycle, phase, reaction and time left, cycle tick marks, standing/total tiles per colour,
layout pick, shrink stage (tiles there / grey / marked), position and tile, FPS, physics ms,
draw calls, triangles, colliders. The same data is JSON in `data-colors` on
`.pl-color-debug` (`shrink.tiles` / `shrink.marks`: one digit per tile). With `?colorDebug=1`: P autopilot for the
local slot, T +5 cycles (faster timers from the next cycle), G background on/off.
