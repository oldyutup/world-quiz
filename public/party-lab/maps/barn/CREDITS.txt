Only this curated subset is included:
  Farm:     Big Barn (its two sliding doors only, for the south entrance)
  Medieval: Package-kYvD6QCQRd (as hay bale), Hay, Barrel, Bags, Fence (deck rail)
  Shooter:  Crate, Pallet, Shotgun, Smg, Bear Trap

Changes: merged into one GLB; only positions, normals and indices kept (the
weapons' all-white vertex colours and unused UVs dropped); every material is an
opaque, metalness-0 flat colour from one palette (the SMG's duplicated
transparent materials merged); props re-pivoted and scaled to gameplay sizes;
Big Barn's doors cut out of the model, re-pivoted and scaled 1.36×. The barn's
walls, roofs, floors and decks are generated in code (the four-wing plan has no
ready-made shell). Gameplay colliders are not derived from
this file (see shared/party-lab/maps/barn.ts). Rebuild with:
  node scripts/build-party-lab-barn-kit.mjs [path/to/PartyLabBarnAssets]
